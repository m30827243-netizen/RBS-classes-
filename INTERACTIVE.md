# RBS Classes — Interactive Learning Universe

## Tagline
Don't Just Learn. Experience It.
Learn → Explore → Practice → Experiment → Play → Apply

## Buildings (Learning World)
- Science Lab: Circuit builder, seed growth, magnet
- Math Lab: Shopping 15% discount, fraction pizza, percent
- Geography: Capitals match, Himalaya climate think
- Civics: CM budget simulator (₹1000)
- Money Lab: ₹30k salary + emergency
- Hindi: Muhavra games
- Game Arena: Science/Math/History rapid quiz
- Life Lab: UPI, ATM, scam, email, first-aid
- Practice Particles: 30s–2min challenges +10 XP
- Missions: Real-world submit → XP
